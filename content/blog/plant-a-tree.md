> _"The best time to plant a tree is 20 years ago. The second-best time is now."_
>
> _-- Dambisa Moyo_

## Why plant a tree

I set up a blog a long time ago, around 2019 maybe? Back then I was just playing around and thought having a blog was cool. I wrote maybe one or two posts and that was it. At the end of last year I rebuilt it on a whim, deployed it to GitHub Pages, and never touched it again 😂.

Today I finally wrote my first post, mostly inspired by a colleague, [Bysir](https://blog.bysir.top/). I stumbled upon his blog and GitHub: he writes now and then and has lots of interesting projects. We've been talking a lot about AI lately, ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion and the like, and it suddenly hit me that I've wasted quite a lot of time. Hence this post.

I used to set plenty of goals and never stuck with any of them. The last one was to build something interesting in Rust; I only got as far as starting it. Before every weekend I'd tell myself "this time for sure", then Saturday came, I got lazy and started gaming. This time I need a real change.

## How to plant a tree

[Bysir](https://blog.bysir.top/) mentioned he had applied for a public IP, so last weekend I called my ISP (China Unicom, 10010) and applied too. They said it needs to go through the local service center and will take about a week. Some goals for now:

- [x] Polish my blog
- [ ] Publish one post every week, any topic
- [x] Install WSL on Windows and host some fun services
- [ ] (For real) build an interesting project in Rust
- [x] Learn Docker and publish an image

That's it for now; I'll add more as they come up. The plan is to finish everything in two months, by the end of April.

Finally, I hope this isn't just talk. Change starts now.
